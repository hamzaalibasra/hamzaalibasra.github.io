Cash Flow for Founders

A Practical System to Get Paid on Time, Stop Living Invoice to Invoice, and Build a 3-Month Runway

THE WORK IS DONE. THE INVOICE IS SENT. SO WHY IS THE ACCOUNT EMPTY?

The clients are happy. The work shipped on time. On paper, it was your best month of the year — and in the bank there is a contractor to pay on Friday and a tax bill sitting in the middle of the month. You do not have a talent problem. You have a cash flow problem — and cash flow is not weather. It is a system. This book installs it.

Cash Flow for Founders is the practical engine room beneath The Self-Made Code: a step-by-step system for getting paid on time, ending the invoice-to-invoice life, and building a three-month cash buffer that turns late payments from emergencies into annoyances.

Inside, you will learn:

  • The deposit rule and the five terms to agree before work starts — so payment becomes a schedule, not a hope
  • The Follow-Up Ladder, plus seven word-for-word scripts for every stage of a late payment
  • Your real number: the floor-rate calculation that starts pricing from your costs instead of your fears
  • The three-account system, the Friday Thirty routine, and the 72-hour Cash Crunch Playbook

Every chapter opens with a founder inside the problem, teaches the system, and closes with Field Rules and a Do This Week checklist. No tricks. No loopholes. No hype. Read it with a pen.

Book 1 · The Self-Made Series

The Self-Made Code

How Founders Build Empires, Decide Under Pressure, and Create Wealth That Lasts

NOBODY HANDS YOU AN EMPIRE.

No inheritance. No connections. No permission slip. If you are building from zero, this is not another motivation book — it is a code: twenty laws for founders who intend to build something real, and keep it.

Every law in The Self-Made Code stands on a true story. James Dyson built 5,127 failed prototypes before his machine worked. Kodak invented the digital camera in 1975 — and chose comfort until the comfort bankrupted it. A janitor died with $8 million. A toy empire with full stores suffocated on arithmetic. These are documented events, not parables — and each one carries a law you can use the same day you read it.

Inside, you will learn:

  • Why comfort charges interest — and how to make discomfort your deposit (Law No. 2)
  • How owners decide under pressure, at the speed of the stakes (Law No. 3)
  • The refusal list that turns scattered effort into strategy (Law No. 7)
  • Cash flow, the three pages of money, and investing like an owner (Laws No. 13–15)
  • Why empires rot from the inside — and the audit that catches it early (Law No. 18)

Every chapter closes with Field Rules and one practical exercise. No theory for theory’s sake. Read it with a pen.
