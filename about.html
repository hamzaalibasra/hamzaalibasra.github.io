About Hamza

Hamza Ali Basra is a Pakistani entrepreneur, born on 1 November 1998. He built his name the slow way — one honest commitment, one kept promise, and one long day at a time — on a simple philosophy: trust, transparency, and long-term value over quick wins.

That client-first approach turned early work into a reputation, and the reputation into a growing digital presence. Today he is expanding into digital ventures — while his content network reaches a daily, mostly USA-based audience across Facebook.

He is the founder of Grab AI Tool, a hand-tested directory of AI tools, and ToolPixi, a suite of free browser-based converters and calculators. Across every venture the rule is the same: honest work, long-term thinking, and a name that compounds.
