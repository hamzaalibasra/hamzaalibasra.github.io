About Hamza

Hamza Ali Basra is a Pakistani entrepreneur, born on 1 November 1998. He built his name the slow way — one honest commitment, one kept promise, and one long day at a time — on a simple philosophy: trust, transparency, and long-term value over quick wins.

That client-first approach turned early work into a reputation, and the reputation into a growing digital presence. Today he is expanding into digital ventures — while his content network reaches a daily, mostly USA-based audience across Facebook.

He is the founder of Grab AI Tool, a hand-tested directory of AI tools, and ToolPixi, a suite of free browser-based converters and calculators. Across every venture the rule is the same: honest work, long-term thinking, and a name that compounds.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Hamza Ali Basra?

Hamza Ali Basra (born 1 November 1998) is a Pakistani entrepreneur. He is the founder of Grab AI Tool and ToolPixi, and the voice behind a motivational media network led by his flagship Facebook page (29K followers) and CEO LEGACY (20K followers). As featured in ValiantCEO Magazine.

What is Grab AI Tool?

Grab AI Tool (grabaitool.com) is a directory that tests, reviews, and compares AI tools — with a tool-finder quiz, side-by-side comparisons, and an AI cost calculator that shows people what they actually spend on AI subscriptions.

What is ToolPixi?

ToolPixi (toolpixi.com) is a suite of free online tools — image and PDF converters, calculators, and everyday utilities — that run entirely in the visitor's browser. No sign-up.

What services does Hamza offer?

Investor pitch deck design, ATS-friendly resume design, and branded social media content packs — each in three packages with clear prices on the Work With Me page, and available to order through his Fiverr page.

Where has Hamza been featured?

ValiantCEO Magazine published a founder interview with Hamza — “Hamza Ali Basra of Grab AI Tool on Turning Complexity into Clarity” — on 27 October 2026.

What does Hamza write about?

His Daily Insights are short essays on entrepreneurship, business mindset, discipline, self-respect, boundaries, and building an audience honestly — published on this website.
