About Hamza

Hamza Ali Basra is a Pakistani entrepreneur and real estate consultant, born on 1 November 1998. He built his career in Lahore's property market, with a special focus on Bahria Town — guiding investors and families through property investment, sales, and development opportunities with a simple philosophy: trust, transparency, and long-term value over quick wins.

That client-first approach turned individual deals into a reputation, and the reputation into a growing digital presence. Today Hamza is based in Los Angeles, California, where he is expanding from real estate into digital ventures — while his content network reaches a daily, mostly USA-based audience across Facebook.

He is the founder of Grab AI Tool, a hand-tested directory of AI tools, and ToolPixi, a suite of free browser-based converters and calculators. Across every venture the rule is the same: honest work, long-term thinking, and a name that compounds.
