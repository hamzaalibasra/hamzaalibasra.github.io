Short Bio

About Hamza

Hamza Ali Basra (born 1 November 1998) is an entrepreneur. He is the founder of Grab AI Tool, a hand-tested directory of AI tools, and ToolPixi, a suite of free browser-based converters and calculators. He also runs a motivational media network — led by his flagship Facebook page (29K followers) and CEO LEGACY (19K followers) — publishing daily English-language content on mindset, discipline, and business for a mostly USA audience.

One line: Hamza Ali Basra is an entrepreneur — founder of Grab AI Tool and ToolPixi, and the voice behind a motivational media network. As featured in ValiantCEO Magazine.
