Boundaries Quotes

Self-respect with an address.

Every founder learns the same hard arithmetic: access is expensive. Every open door is a claim on your time, your attention, and your peace — and not everyone who asks for access has earned it. The quotes in this collection were written for the Hamza Ali Basra page, where boundaries have been a founding theme from the very first posts.

They cover the whole craft of the closed door: why privacy is power, why guilt is a broken compass, why closure is a decision you make alone instead of a conversation you wait for, and why not every battle deserves you. None of this is coldness. A boundary is simply self-respect with an address — it tells the world where you end and your obligations begin. If your life feels crowded by other people's emergencies, start here, and start closing doors politely.
