NOBODY HANDS YOU AN EMPIRE.

No inheritance. No connections. No permission slip. If you are building from zero, this is not another motivation book — it is a code: twenty laws for founders who intend to build something real, and keep it.

Every law in The Self-Made Code stands on a true story. James Dyson built 5,127 failed prototypes before his machine worked. Kodak invented the digital camera in 1975 — and chose comfort until the comfort bankrupted it. A janitor died with $8 million. A toy empire with full stores suffocated on arithmetic. These are documented events, not parables — and each one carries a law you can use the same day you read it.

Inside, you will learn:

  • Why comfort charges interest — and how to make discomfort your deposit (Law No. 2)
  • How owners decide under pressure, at the speed of the stakes (Law No. 3)
  • The refusal list that turns scattered effort into strategy (Law No. 7)
  • Cash flow, the three pages of money, and investing like an owner (Laws No. 13–15)
  • Why empires rot from the inside — and the audit that catches it early (Law No. 18)

Every chapter closes with Field Rules and one practical exercise. No theory for theory’s sake. Read it with a pen.
